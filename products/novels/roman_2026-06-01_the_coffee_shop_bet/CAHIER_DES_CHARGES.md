## 6. Plan des 38 Chapitres
**Ch.1** : The Unlucky Spill
  > Clara Maxwell, eternally optimistic, starts her new job as CEO Oliver Vance's assistant. Her first interaction involves spilling coffee all over him, immediately establishing his grumpy demeanor.
**Ch.2** : The CEO's Reputation
  > Oliver reflects on his carefully cultivated image of a ruthless, emotionless CEO. He's annoyed by Clara's cheerfulness but finds her surprisingly competent.
**Ch.3** : The Bet Is On
  > After a week of Clara's attempts to bring joy to the office, Oliver, exasperated, proposes the bet: make him smile in a week, or she's out. Clara, ever confident, accepts.
**Ch.4** : Office Pranks and Puns
  > Clara starts her 'smile campaign' with silly office pranks, dad jokes, and cheerful decor. Oliver is immune, but grudgingly amused.
**Ch.5** : The Morning Ritual
  > Clara tries to disrupt Oliver's stoic morning routine with upbeat music and gourmet coffee. He remains unfazed, but she notices subtle changes.
**Ch.6** : A Glimpse Behind the Grump
  > During a late-night work session, Oliver accidentally reveals a small personal detail about his past, showing a crack in his armor. Clara listens without judgment.
**Ch.7** : The Charity Gala
  > Clara accompanies Oliver to a dull charity gala. Her bubbly personality unexpectedly charms important clients, much to Oliver's surprise and a hint of pride.
**Ch.8** : Unexpected Laughter
  > Clara accidentally spills food on a rival CEO, leading to a genuinely funny moment where Oliver almost, *almost*, cracks a smile.
**Ch.9** : The Bet's First Week Ends
  > The week is over, and Oliver hasn't officially smiled. Clara is disappointed but not defeated. Oliver feels a pang of something he can't name.
**Ch.10** : A New Deal
  > Oliver, realizing he doesn't want Clara to leave, extends the bet with new terms: she stays, and the challenge continues indefinitely. He subtly makes it clear he wants her to stay.
**Ch.11** : Working Late, Getting Close
